## 🚀 Key Features
- **Agency Typography & Layout**: Built with high-craft editorial serif (`Cormorant Garamond`) paired with `Plus Jakarta Sans` and `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Tri-Continental Footprint**: Interactive showcases of operations across East Africa, North America, and South Asia.
- **Dynamic Filtering**: Filter mandates by International Business, Community Governance, and Education Trusts.
- **Direct vCard Generator**: 1-click download of `abdul-salam.vcf` for contact books.
- **Dual Luxury Mode**: Obsidian Dark and Ivory Alabaster theme toggle.
- **SEO & AI AEO Ready**: Full JSON-LD Schema graph (`Person`, `ProfilePage`, `FAQPage`), `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, and `/llms.txt` for AI answer engines.
