Abdulsalam Abdulrahman (B.Com, LL.B, MBA) is a multi-disciplinary corporate chairman, legal counsel, and strategic advisor based in Kigali, Rwanda. He guides institutional capital deployment, cross-border corporate law compliance, and public-private-community partnerships across East Africa, India, and the United States.

He currently serves as Executive Chairman of Bell Africa Group of Companies PLC (Ethiopia), Chairman of Sasco Industries Ltd (Rwanda), Chairman of Apar Concepts PLC (California, USA), Chairman of Future Vision Asset Management Ltd, Mullassery Mushrooms, Kairali Organics, and Ideal Rubbers Ltd (India), as well as Chief Advisor for Development to TUZAMURANE Cooperative Society (Rwanda).

With his formal corporate legal training (LL.B) and commercial grounding (B.Com, MBA), Abdul Salam acts as a primary institutional liaison. He navigates complex regulatory frameworks, mitigates local operational and supply chain risks, ensures government alignment, and secures grassroots cooperative buy-in for foreign investors.

As Chief Advisor for Development to the TUZAMURANE Cooperative Society in Rwanda, he provides strategic guidance on sustainable development planning, capacity building, and economic self-reliance, helping transform grassroots farming initiatives into commercially viable export entities.

Abdulsalam Abdulrahman holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA), a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B) focusing on corporate jurisprudence and international commercial law, and a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) in financial strategy and accounting.
